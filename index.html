Transmission Line Sending-End Parameter Analyzer

Generalized Problem Statement

"A 50 Hz transmission line L km long has a series impedance of Z ohm/km and a total shunt admittance of Y mho. The receiving-end load is PR MW at VR kV with m lagging power factor. Write a generalized program create a portal to calculate voltage, current, power and power factor at the sending-end. Also calculate the voltage regulation of the line and transmission efficiency."

Transmission Line Classification Logic:

  • Short Line: Length < 80 km (Ignores shunt admittance. A=1, B=Z, C=0, D=1).
  • Medium Line: 80 ≤ Length ≤ 250 km (Uses Nominal π or T model based on user selection/default).
  • Long Line: Length > 250 km (Uses exact distributed parameter model with hyperbolic functions).
