Transmission Line Sending-End Parameter Analyzer

Generalized Problem Statement

"A 50 Hz transmission line L km long has a series impedance of Z ohm/km and a total shunt admittance of Y mho. The receiving-end load is PR MW at VR kV with m power factor. Calculate voltage, current, power and power factor at the sending-end. Also calculate the voltage regulation of the line and transmission efficiency."

Transmission Line Classification Logic:

  • Short Line: Length < 80 km (Shunt admittance neglected)
  • Medium Line: 80 ≤ Length ≤ 250 km (Nominal π model)
  • Long Line: Length > 250 km (Exact distributed parameter model)
